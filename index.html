How to play

Choose 2 teams (9 + 8 learners) or 3 teams (6 + 6 + 5). The teacher projects this screen. Each student writes their own answer before discussing it with their team.

  • For every file, nominate a different spokesperson from each team.
  • Give teams roughly 4–5 minutes to solve each file. Use START to run the timer.
  • Press REVEAL TEACHER KEY only after the teams submit their answers.
  • Score with +1 / −1 beneath the team names. Each file has its own maximum.
  • The scoreboard counts team points across all six files.
  • Answers must use the vocabulary and grammar taught in the prepared NPBHP1 lessons; sensible grammatical alternatives are accepted.
  • No extra printouts, accounts, or online assets are necessary.
